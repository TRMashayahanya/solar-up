Install Solar Up

Use it like an app on iPhone or Android — free, from your browser.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open your Solar Up link in Safari (not WhatsApp’s browser).
  2. Tap Share (square with arrow at the bottom).
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add — the Solar Up icon appears on your home screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open your Solar Up link in Chrome.
  2. If you see Install app at the bottom of the site, tap it.
  3. Or tap menu ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm — Solar Up opens from your home screen like other apps.
